About

SD Therapeutic Massage is a mobile massage practice serving San Diego. Instead of asking you to travel to a spa, everything comes to you — the table or chair, the linens, and a calm, unhurried session in a space where you already feel comfortable.

With a client-first approach, every appointment is adapted to how your body feels that day. Whether you want focused work on stubborn shoulder tension or a full-body reset, the session is built around you.
